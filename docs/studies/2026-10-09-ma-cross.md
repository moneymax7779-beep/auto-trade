# EMA9 / EMA20 cross with a swing stop (ledger A-066): registered before any replay

User's request, 9 Oct 2026 ("do both, register and test it"), after a YouTube strategy: EMA 9 crossing SMA 21 -> trade
in the cross's direction, stop at the recent swing, target 2x the stop distance.

## Prior screen (not evidence: 5 own-capture days, 27 signals, option mids at the index-level touch)
On 28, 29, 30 Sep, 1 and 5 Oct: cross up/down of the features' EMA9 over EMA20 (3-minute bars), stop at the last swing
low/high, target 2x risk, at most 60 minutes: 27 signals, 52 % wins, +10.9 % of premium per trade after 0.3 % costs,
profit factor 2.41, positive on all 5 days. With a 1-ATR stop instead of the swing: PF 0.89. The edge, if any, sits in
the swing stop. The rule is now tested properly, with the executor (fills, lot rounding, the liquidity cap).

## Rule, v1 (`ma-cross.v1.yaml`), fixed now; every value a round number
1. **Signal**: this minute's EMA9 - EMA20 (features, 3-minute bars) has the opposite sign to the previous minute's: a
   cross up -> call, a cross down -> put. Entries 09:30-14:30. Never the index that expires that day.
2. **Stop**: the last swing low (calls) / high (puts) from the features; skip when it is unknown, on the wrong side, or
   more than 3 ATR(3m) away.
3. **Target**: entry + 2 x (entry - stop) for calls; mirrored for puts.
4. **Exits**: index at the target or the stop; 60 minutes; 15:15. Resting premium stop 25 % (emergency).
5. **Size**: Rs 2,50,000 of ATM premium, one entry per cross (the SetupStrategy frame).

## Test
Standalone replays, features v11, the live risk file (v12: equity = starting capital in replay, liquidity cap on).
- Test: 3, 8, 10, 15, 17 Sep (zt archive, Mac); 28, 29, 30 Sep, 1 Oct, 5 Oct (own capture, GCP).
- Held-out: 22, 24 Sep, read once. 6 Oct: part of neither (its mids were in the screen only indirectly); reported apart.
Note the screen's 5 days are among the test days: the test is therefore partly in-sample; the held-out days decide.

**PASS** only if, on the test days: at least 20 trades, net > 0, profit factor >= 1.3, no day below -Rs 1,10,000; and
on the held-out days net >= 0. Fewer than 20 test trades = INCONCLUSIVE. A PASS means a forward test in PAPER alongside
the live set only if the user decides so (it would share the capital).
