# Gap-and-go at the open (registered 2026-09-29, before computing)

Question (user, 2026-09-29, after NIFTY opened below the previous day's low and fell 151 points by
09:38 while the opening drive — which excludes gaps by design — stood aside): when the index opens
beyond the previous day's range and the first minutes keep going the gap's way, does buying the ATM
option in the gap's direction pay? **29 Sep is the design day: reported, not counted.**

## Data (zt-tiger-v2, read-only, after market hours)
As docs/studies/2026-09-28-opening-drive.md: spot 1-minute bars (MULTI_TICK), previous-session levels
from BROKER_HISTORICAL_BACKFILL (all 360 bars) else MULTI_TICK (≥ 355 bars incl. 09:15 and 15:14),
option snapshots of the nearest expiry (about one a minute, bid/ask). Sessions 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11,
15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 28 Sep (test) + 29 Sep (design); NIFTY and SENSEX.

## Rule (fixed)
- Gap down: the day's first 1-minute bar (09:15) opens below the previous session's low (PDL) → put
  side. Gap up: it opens above the previous session's high (PDH) → call side. Otherwise no trade.
- Go (continuation): the first 1-minute bar starting 09:16–09:24 that closes beyond the 09:15 bar's
  extreme in the gap's direction (put: close < the 09:15 bar's low; call: close > its high). One trade
  per index-day.
- Entry: the first option snapshot 0–90 s after that bar closes; strike = the snapshot's ATM strike;
  buy at the ask (bid > 0, ask ≥ bid).
- Exits (first to happen; sell at the snapshot bid):
  1. Gap failing: a 1-minute bar closes back at or beyond the day's open (put: close ≥ open).
  2. Premium stop: bid ≤ 0.75 × entry ask.
  3. Trailing stop: once the best bid since entry reaches 1.2 × entry ask, bid ≤ 0.8 × that best bid.
  4. Time: first snapshot at or after 10:00:00.
  (Exits 2–4 as the opening drive; exit 1 is the gap's own failure point.)
- Costs v1, one lot (NIFTY 65, SENSEX 20).

## Data-quality rules (dropped and counted, never patched)
- Index-day dropped if a MULTI_TICK bar is missing 09:15–10:00, there is no option snapshot at the
  open, or the previous session's levels fail the rule above.
- Trade dropped if the traded strike has a snapshot gap over 90 s between entry and exit, or no entry
  snapshot within 90 s.

## Reported
Per trade (index, day, side, gap size, trigger, strike, entry, exit, reason, return, net per lot);
count, wins, mean, median, total; gap days without a "go"; dropped index-days; the design day apart.

## What counts
Descriptive: at most ~36 index-days, all seen before. A positive result would justify forward PAPER
testing, not a conclusion.

## Reading
Gap days are common here (16 of 26 usable index-days), but after a gap and a first continuation bar
the index more often filled back than kept going; the ATM option lost on 5 of 6 test trades. Today's
+₹2,700 per lot was the exception, not the pattern. Not a strategy; not deployed.
