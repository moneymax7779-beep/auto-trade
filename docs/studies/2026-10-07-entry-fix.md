# Entry fix: entries that keep up with a moving option (risk v11, ledger A-055), registered before any result

User, 7 Oct 2026 16:40 IST, after the day's review: "yes do the entry fix and test it".

## The problem (7 Oct live, session 641)
Two of six break-retest entries never filled. Each was a marketable limit at the ask seen at the decision plus 4 ticks
(₹0.20). In the ~0.3 s before the order was accepted, the option's ask rose about ₹1 (0.3 %) in the trade's direction
and never came back within the 10 s timeout:
- 11:39 SENSEX 72900 CE: limit ₹290.55, ask ₹291.45 at acceptance, ₹291.40–295.20 for 10 s;
- 14:05 SENSEX 72600 PE: limit ₹310.80, ask ₹311.75 at acceptance, ₹311.60–315.00 for 10 s.
Entries that miss this way are the ones whose option moves the trade's way immediately, so a systematic miss biases
which signals get traded. Straddle legs already re-price (their own chase); single-leg entries did not.

## The change (execution only; no strategy's signals change)
risk v11 = v10 plus: entry limit = ask + max(4 ticks, 0.3 % of the ask); an entry still unfilled 2 s after it was
priced is re-priced once at the current ask + that buffer (only ever raising the limit); the 10 s timeout counts from
the first send. `paper-risk.v11.yaml`, `RiskLimits.entryBuffer`, `OrderManager.chaseEntry`.

## Test (GCP, after the A-048/A-049 and A-052 batches, 7 Oct evening)
The live set (7 strategies), costs v2, features v11, one image; risk v10 vs v11, otherwise identical:
- test days, own capture: 28, 29, 30 Sep, 1, 5, 6, 7 Oct;
- held-out, zt-tiger-v2: 22, 23, 24, 25 Sep, read once for this hypothesis.
The paper broker models the same 250 ms latency on recorded quotes, so the misses reproduce in replay.

**PASS if all hold:** (1) over the test days v11 has fewer unfilled entries than v10; (2) the extra paid per filled
entry (fill vs the ask seen at the decision) averages ≤ 0.5 % of the premium; (3) v11's test-day net ≥ v10's − ₹25,000
(more fills may add losers as well as winners; the fix must not make the set materially worse); (4) held-out: v11's
net ≥ v10's − ₹25,000. A PASS means switching live to risk v11.
