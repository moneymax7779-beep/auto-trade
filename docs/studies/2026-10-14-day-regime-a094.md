# Day regime engine, step 1: can the application read the day's trend? (ledger A-094)

Registered 14 Oct 2026, before any model is fitted. User: "application should understand the trend of the market that
day" (INTELLIGENT-TRADING-PLAN.md phase 3), then "yes register A-094 and start step 1".

## Why

- **The 14 Oct loss-day review (`.local/loss-days`).** On six losing bar days ma-cross took 26 trades and won 2. Puts
  were bought on up days (10 Apr, 20 May), and calls and puts alternated on narrow days (8 May, 7 Aug).
- **Price-only baseline (`.local/regime/baseline.py`).** Measured on 372 index-days, 2 Jan–5 Oct, from hist.candle
  index bars, with the VWAP equal-weighted because the index has no volume. The persistence rules were the side of the
  open, the side of VWAP, an opening-range break held, a move larger than 0.3 × the prior range, and all of them
  agreeing.
  - Checked at 09:30, 10:15, 11:00, 12:00 and 13:00, these rules call the direction of the rest of the day 47–57 % of
    the time.
  - A strong 11:00 state ends as a trend the opposite way only 1–3 % of the time.
  - Day types at the close: two-way 50 %, trend down 22 %, trend up 17 %, narrow 10 %.

The question is whether adding flow inputs (futures OI and volume, option OI, IV, VIX, the other index) to price makes
the day readable. If it does, a regime engine can tell the strategies which side to trade and when to stand aside. If
it does not, the regime idea stops here.

## Data

**Step 1** stores the per-minute feature snapshot of every replayed day, under a `research.run` of kind
`REPLAY_FEATURES`. This needs the new replay option `--autotrade.trading.replay-snapshots=true`.
- The snapshots come from the same engine and features file as live (features v12).
- Nothing reads `REPLAY_FEATURES` runs except this study. IV history and the chart levels read `LIVE_FEATURES` only.

**Splits, fixed now:**

| Set | Days | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Train | bar days 2 Jan–30 Jun 2026 | Fitting. The regularisation is chosen by walk-forward by month inside train (fit on months before, score on the next). |
| Test | bar days 1 Jul–31 Aug 2026 | Never used for fitting or choices. Read once. |
| Final | the 24 tick days, 2 Sep–13 Oct 2026, replayed from captured ticks (zt archive / own capture) | Read once, after the test. These days carry the live feature definitions (captured futures volume, IV, breadth). |
| Excluded | bar days in September | Same market days as the tick days. |

**Bar-day caveats:**
- There is no breadth before July. Breadth is not an input.
- Futures volume and RVOL on bar days come from minute bars, while live uses captured ticks. RVOL is therefore not an
  input; futures OI is.

## Labels (fixed now)

- **L1, primary:** the direction of the rest of the day from checkpoint T. `sign(close at 15:29 − spot at T)`, for
  T = 10:15, 11:00 and 12:00, with one row per index, day and checkpoint.
- **L2, secondary:** the day type at the close, defined as in the baseline.
  - TREND_UP: |close − open| ≥ 0.5 × range and the close in the top 25 % of the range.
  - TREND_DOWN: the mirror of TREND_UP.
  - NARROW: range < 0.6 × the previous day's range.
  - TWO_WAY: everything else.
- **L3, secondary:** which comes first within 60 minutes of T, the index at spot + 0.25 × the previous day's range or
  at spot − 0.25 × that range. Neither is its own class.

## Model (fixed now)

L2-regularised logistic regression, one model per label and checkpoint, with inputs standardised per index on train
only. A gradient-boosted tree model may be reported beside it as a challenger, but the verdict is the logistic
model's.

**Inputs:** at most these 18, all read from the snapshot at T, plus the other index's value at T.

| Group | Inputs |
|---|---|
| Price | 1. (spot − dayOpen) / prior range · 2. (dayOpen − prevClose) / prior range (gap) · 3. distVwapAtr · 4. distOrhAtr · 5. distOrlAtr · 6. distEma20Atr · 7. ema9Slope · 8. location in the day's range so far · 9. compression.vwapCrosses · 10. regime.dayRangeVsExpected |
| Futures | 11. futures.oiChangeDay / futures.oi |
| Options | 12. options.peOiChange10m − options.ceOiChange10m (standardised per index) · 13. options.atmIvChange5m · 14. options.ivSkew · 15. options.straddleChange5m |
| Volatility | 16. volatility.vixChangeDay |
| Other index | 17. the other index's (spot − dayOpen) / prior range |
| Calendar | 18. expiry day for this index (regime.dteCalendarDays = 0) |

**Baseline:** the best of the five price-only rules on the train months at that checkpoint.

## Pass criterion (fixed now)

**PASS** needs all of the following.

1. **Test (Jul–Aug bar days), L1 at 11:00:**
   - accuracy ≥ 60 % and ≥ the baseline + 5 points;
   - the Brier score is below the train base rate's.
2. **Test, L1 at 10:15 and 12:00:** accuracy not below the baseline.
3. **Test, confident rows:** where P(direction) ≥ 0.65 at 11:00 there are at least 20 rows, and the realised hit rate
   is ≥ 60 %.
4. **Final (tick days), L1 at 11:00:** accuracy ≥ 55 % and not below the baseline on the same days.

- **FAIL:** any of the above misses. A FAIL is recorded, and the regime engine is not built further on these inputs.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** fewer than 60 test rows at 11:00.

**Reported but not gated:**
- L2 and L3 accuracy and calibration.
- A descriptive look at ma-cross, odm and orb trades with P(the trade's direction) below 0.4 at entry.

A PASS puts the regime engine into shadow only: a display on the chart, a Telegram line and a field in the candidate
context. Any gating of strategies is a later ledger row and the user's decision.

## Result (14 Oct 2026, 01:51–02:31 IST; Mac, image f75550eb4877-a94; `.local/a94/`)

**Run checks:**
- 149 bar days (23 Jan–31 Aug) and 24 tick days were replayed with snapshots. 1 Feb, the Sunday budget session, is not
  a trading day in the exchange calendar.
- Every day's net equals the earlier f75550 all-days run, so storing snapshots changed no trade.
- 128,657 minute rows. No input was missing on the train days.
- Rows per checkpoint: 208 train, 88 test and 48 final index-days.

**Train only (walk-forward by month):**
- The best regularisation is the strongest one tried (C = 0.01), and even that has a log-loss of 0.70–0.71, worse than
  a constant 50 % (0.693).
- The price rules on train score 49–57 %.

**Test (Jul–Aug, read once):**

| Checkpoint | Model | Best price rule (side of VWAP) | Brier vs base rate | Rows with P ≥ 0.65 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10:15 | 50.0 % | 51.1 % | 0.2500 vs 0.2500 | 1 |
| 11:00 | 64.8 % | 52.3 % | 0.2439 vs 0.2462 | 0 |
| 12:00 | 56.8 % | 43.2 % | 0.2466 vs 0.2482 | 8 (hit 37.5 %) |

**Final (24 tick days, read once):**

| Checkpoint | Model | Side of VWAP |
|---|---|---|
| 10:15 | 52.1 % | 66.7 % |
| 11:00 | 60.4 % | 66.7 % |
| 12:00 | 62.5 % | 64.6 % |

The side-of-VWAP rule is unstable between periods: 56 % on train, 43–52 % on test, 65–67 % on the tick days.

**Secondary:** the day type (L2) and the first ±0.25-range move within 60 minutes (L3) are no better than the majority
class on test.

**Descriptive:** trades the model called against the day (P(trade direction) < 0.4 at the last checkpoint before the
entry) did not do worse.
- Test, ma-cross: 12 trades, PF 3.81.
- Test, ma-cross with P > 0.6: 8 trades, PF 0.16.
- Final: PF 1.43 to 5.11 in every band.

**Verdict: FAIL** (criteria 2, 3 and 4). The 11:00 test accuracy passed criterion 1. Its probabilities stay within a
few points of 50 %, and it did not hold up on the tick days, so it is not a usable signal.

**What it means:**
- With these inputs (price structure, futures OI, option OI change, IV, skew, straddle, VIX, the other index), the
  rest-of-day direction from 10:15–12:00 is not predictable on these 172 days. Intraday index direction after the
  first hour behaves close to a random walk at this horizon.
- A regime engine built on these inputs would add noise. The capture gains have to come from execution and exits
  (holding winners, cutting chop), not from calling the day.
- Inputs not tried here: breadth (only from July), order-book depth (tick days only), BANKNIFTY, global cues. Any new
  attempt is a new ledger row.
