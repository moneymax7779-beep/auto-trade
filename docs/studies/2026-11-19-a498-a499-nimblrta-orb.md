# A-498 / A-499 — NimblrTA opening-range breakout on the index, and the "range left" filter (19 Nov 2026)

Source: the Udemy course "Opening Range Breakout Strategy using Candlesticks" (NimblrTA, 2290263). The course notes
are in `docs/research/udemy/nimblrta-orb-candlesticks.md`. The user asked on 19 Nov: "Yes, N1 + N3".

Both tests were registered in `docs/EXPERIMENT-LEDGER.md` before any script was written or any bar was read.

**Both FAIL.** The course's filters make each trade better, but they do not add total profit over a plain
5-minute ORB. The "range left" filter is backwards: the late, big-range breaks are the best trades.

## Data and method

| Item | Detail |

| Checks done | Every tick day starts 09:15 with ≥ 370 minutes; ADR20 and morning ranges spot-checked (e.g. NIFTY 6 Nov: ADR20 311 pts, range to 09:50 257 pts, day 642 pts) |

## A-498 — the course's rules (CV) vs a plain 5-minute ORB (PB), same exits

**What the two arms do:**
- **CV (course version):**
  - two strength candles: #1 closes beyond the body line, #2 closes beyond the wick line, each making a new day
    extreme;
  - then CCI34 on 30 minutes beyond ±100 and EMA 5 / 21 / 34 stacked;
  - entry on the 5-minute close.
- **PB (plain baseline):** the first 5-minute close beyond the 09:15–09:30 high or low.
- **Shared rules:**
  - exits: the trailing stop 0.234 % beyond the last indecisive 30-minute candle; the dynamic exit (CCI34 on
    30 minutes < +110 and CCI34 on 5 minutes < −60, mirrored for sells); flat at 15:15;
  - entries until 14:30; one per direction per index per day.

| **CV** | 41 | 27 | **+7.81 %** | **3.23** | **+0.191 %** | +6.52 % (19) | +1.30 % (22) | +5.42 % |
| PB | 92 | 45 | +8.64 % | 1.59 | +0.094 % | +6.07 % (48) | +2.57 % (44) | +5.46 % |

- **By index (CV):** NIFTY 22 trades, +950 pts, PF 2.99; SENSEX 19 trades, +2,695 pts, PF 3.60.
- **CV exits:** dynamic exit 10, trailing stop 13, 15:15 flat 18.
- **CV entry times:** 20 of 41 entries came by 10:10; the earliest at 09:40.
- **The course's trailing stop is wide:** about 0.4–2 % of the index at entry (≈ 100–450 NIFTY points).
- **Exit variant (descriptive):** the CCI8 exit printed on the course slide is worse: CV +4.74 % (H2 −0.85 %),
  PB +5.83 % (H2 −1.23 %).

**Verdict: FAIL as registered.**
- CV met every criterion except one. Both halves positive ✓; positive without the best day ✓; PF ≥ 1.3 ✓; mean ≥
  0.10 % ✓.
- But it **did not beat the plain ORB in H2** (−1.27 %).

**What the numbers do say.** The filters halve the number of trades and double the average move per trade
(0.19 % vs 0.09 %), and only CV clears the rough option-cost hurdle of 0.10 %. Per trade, the course's
confirmation is better; in total, it is not. Two cautions:
1. These 36 days are a strong trending month (NIFTY 22,400 → 25,400). Every ORB construction has been positive on
   these days and negative on 2024–25 bars (A-082 / A-409 vs A-173 / A-414).
2. Index points are not option P&L.

## A-499 — "range left" filter: skip a break when the day has used ≥ 0.6 of its 20-day average range

- **Measure:** used = (day high − day low from 09:15 up to the entry minute) ÷ ADR20.
- **Origin of the 0.6:** the course's stock example (expected range ~2.6 %, enter only with ≥ 1 % left).
- **Lists it was applied to:** brtr-v1's 79 trades in the A-404 / A-405 live-set replay (rupees), and the two
  A-498 lists (% of the index).

| List | Trades | Net by used: < 0.4 | 0.4–0.6 | 0.6–0.8 | ≥ 0.8 | Skipped (wins / losses) | Net before → after | Gain H1 / H2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **brtr-v1 (live)** | 79 | −24K (15) | +2.09 L (27) | +4.70 L (27) | −22K (10) | 7 winners +7.20 L / 30 losers −2.72 L | +6.32 L → +1.84 L | −2.86 L / −1.62 L |
| A-498 CV | 41 | +1.08 % (5) | −1.57 % (14) | −0.32 % (2) | **+8.64 % (20, 18 wins)** | 18 / 4 | +7.81 % → −0.50 % | −5.88 % / −2.44 % |
| A-498 PB | 92 | +1.05 % (20) | +0.76 % (37) | +3.64 % (27) | +3.19 % (8) | 21 / 14 | +8.64 % → +1.81 % | −7.14 % / +0.32 % |

**Verdict: FAIL on all three lists.**
- The filter mostly removes winners: on brtr-v1, 7 skipped winners are worth +7.20 L against 2.72 L of losses
  avoided.
- A big morning range marks a trend day, and trend days are where breakouts pay. A-411 found the same: the money is
  on the trend days the set catches.
- The course's rule is built for stocks with a fixed daily-range target; on these indices it points the wrong way.

**Not a result (post hoc):** the reverse rule — take CV breaks only when used ≥ 0.8 — shows 20 trades with 18 wins
on these days. It was found by looking at this data, so it would need its own registration and data it has not
seen. The 2024–26 Upstox INDEX bars in `hist.candle` (1 Jan 2024 – 6 Oct 2026) would serve. Per the standing rule,
those are bar days and need the user's go-ahead first.

## What this means for our ORB work

- **No change to any live or shadow strategy.**
- The course's two-candle + CCI + EMA confirmation improves trade quality but not net, on days where everything ORB
  works anyway.
- The honest next test of CV is not more tick days; it is the 2024–25 bars, where every ORB has failed before.
  Only after that would an option-price engine replay be worth building.
- The "range left" idea should not be added anywhere.

## A-500 — the course version and the "late break" variant on 2024–26 Upstox index bars (registered after A-498 / A-499)

- **Sample:** 657 bar days per index, 1 Feb 2024 – 25 Sep 2026, NIFTY + SENSEX. Same code as A-498 (`.local/a500/bars.py`
  imports `.local/a498/nimblr_orb.py` unchanged).
- **Periods:** P1 = 2024, P2 = 2025, P3 = 2026 to 18 Sep.
- **Held-out 22–25 Sep:** read once and reported apart.

| Arm | Trades | Win % | Net (% of index) | PF | Mean / trade | P1 | P2 | P3 | Held-out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CV (course) | 641 | 49 % | +37.51 % | 1.48 | +0.059 % | +19.00 % (0.083 %) | +13.07 % (0.053 %) | +5.44 % (0.033 %) | −0.31 % (5) |
| PB (plain ORB) | 1,633 | 39 % | +26.96 % | 1.14 | +0.017 % | +19.38 % | +7.24 % | +0.35 % | +0.93 % (9) |
| CV-late (used ≥ 0.8) | 231 | 53 % | +16.99 % | 1.51 | +0.074 % | +9.53 % (0.110 %) | +4.73 % (0.054 %) | +2.73 % (0.049 %) | +0.01 % (3) |
| PB-late (descriptive) | 162 | 45 % | +8.93 % | 1.32 | +0.055 % | +16.60 % | −5.19 % | −2.49 % | −0.20 % (2) |

**Verdict: FAIL on both questions.**
1. **CV:** its mean of 0.059 % per trade is below the 0.10 % cost hurdle, and it trails the plain ORB in 2024 by
   0.38 %.
2. **CV-late:** its mean is 0.074 %. The 18-of-20 on the tick days becomes 53 % wins on the bars, so it was a
   tick-day accident.

What is still worth knowing:
- In index terms, the course's confirmation is the first ORB form here that is positive in every bar period, and
  it stays positive without each period's best day. Its edge per trade is about 3.5× the plain ORB's.
- But the edge shrinks every year: 0.083 % → 0.053 % → 0.033 %.
- At around 15 NIFTY points per trade, held up to the whole session, a bought ATM option would give it back in
  spread and theta.
- No option-priced engine replay is justified.
